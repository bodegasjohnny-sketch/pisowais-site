Privacy notice

Short version: this website does not collect or store your personal data. Last updated 2026-11-04.

What this site collects

  • No accounts, no database. This is a static website. There is no server of ours that receives form data.
  • No analytics or trackers. We do not use cookies, Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel or similar tools.
  • Calculators and quiz: everything you type is processed only inside your browser and is gone when you close the page.
  • Email field: it is not saved by this site. If you use it, your own email app opens with a pre-written message. Your email reaches us only if you choose to send it.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may log technical data such as IP addresses for security and operations. See GitHub's privacy statement for details.

If you email or message us

We use your email or message only to reply and, if you ask, to send Piso Wais updates. We do not sell or share it. You can ask us to delete it anytime. These rights come from the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173): to be informed, to access, to object, to correct, to erasure, and to file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission.

Links to other sites

Buttons to banks and e-wallets take you to their own sites, which have their own privacy policies.
