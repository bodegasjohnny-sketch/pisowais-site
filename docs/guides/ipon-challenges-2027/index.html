Savings, with generator

Ipon challenges for 2027: pick one, print it, start in January

Bagong taon, bagong ipon. Pero yung kaya mong tapusin.

An ipon challenge turns saving into a weekly habit. Below are three styles and a generator that builds your 2027 schedule by week. Choose the one that fits your income, not the one that looks impressive online.

Piso Wais, published Nov 4, 2026

1

Lesson 1 Free

The classic 52-week, ascending

Week 1 you save 1 unit, week 2 saves 2 units, up to week 52. It starts easy and gets heavier near December, which is also when expenses spike.

Example only. Unit ₱20: week 1 ₱20, week 52 ₱1,040. Total after 52 weeks: 20 × (1 + 2 + ... + 52) = 20 × 1,378 = ₱27,560.

2

Lesson 2 Free

Reverse 52-week, descending

Start with the biggest week in January, when 13th month and bonuses may still be around, and end with the smallest in December. Same total, easier finish.

3

Lesson 3 Free

Flat weekly

The same amount every week. Simple and predictable, good for fixed salaries.

Example only. ₱300 a week for 52 weeks = ₱15,600.

4

Lesson 4 Free

Make it stick

Use a separate savings account or pocket, not a coin jar you can easily open. Set a weekly reminder on the same day as payday. If you miss a week, double the next one or simply continue. Done is better than perfect.

5

Lesson 5 Free

Give the money a job

Name the goal before you start: emergency fund, tuition, a new laptop for work. A named goal is easier to protect.

6

Lesson 6 Free

Choosing the right size

Pick a weekly amount you could still save in your worst normal week. If the challenge only works in good weeks, it will break the first time something goes wrong. It is better to finish a small challenge than to quit a big one.

For irregular income, choose the flat style with a modest amount, then add extra in good weeks as a bonus.

Example only. A tricycle driver in Tarlac with uneven daily income picks a flat ₱100 a week, and adds ₱50 extra in weeks with a big fiesta or school opening rush.

7

Lesson 7 Free

Do it with a buddy

Saving with a sibling, partner or officemate helps. Share a screenshot every Sunday, not the amount, just a check mark. Accountability without pressure.

8

Lesson 8 Free

What to do at the end

Before week 52, decide where the total goes: emergency fund top-up, a goal, or the next challenge. Moving it right away protects it from holiday spending.

FAQ

When does the generator start?

On Monday, January 4, 2027, the first Monday of the year. Each row is the week starting that date.

Can I do bi-weekly?

Yes. Set weeks to 26 and use your payday as the start.

Is my schedule saved?

No. Everything is computed in your browser. Print or screenshot it.
