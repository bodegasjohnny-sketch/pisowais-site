Where to park idle money

Ten Philippine e-wallet savings products and digital banks, sorted by base rate. Every rate shows the date we checked it (2026-11-04) and links to the official page.

Educational only, not financial advice. Rates change, check the app.

How we are paid: right now, nothing. The "Open account" buttons go to each platform's official site. If we later switch a button to a referral link, it will show an Affiliate link tag and we may receive a reward if you sign up. That never changes the rates or the order, which is sorted by base rate only.
Two things before you move money: (1) An e-wallet balance is not a bank deposit. Only money in the partner bank savings account is a deposit. (2) PDIC insures deposits up to PHP 1,000,000 per depositor, per bank. Spreading large amounts across banks keeps each one under the cap. Interest is subject to 20% final withholding tax.
