Privacy notice

Choose what you share. Dashboard accounts are coming soon; the review preview does not connect to a cloud account database. Updated November 7, 2026.

Free downloads, calculators and quizzes

The cheat sheet requires no email, account or payment. Calculator and quiz inputs are processed in your browser. We do not collect those numbers. No analytics, advertising pixels or financial-input logging is included.

Dashboard examples

The dashboard’s example mode uses fictional, clearly labelled amounts. Edits are kept only in the open page’s memory and reset on reload. It is not an account.

Optional local preview

If you explicitly choose the local preview, entries are saved in this browser’s local storage. They are not encrypted, uploaded, synced or backed up. Anyone using this browser may see them. Avoid sensitive real numbers on shared devices. Clearing browser data loses the entries. Use “Your data” → “Clear local preview data” to remove the preview records. Local preview data is never automatically imported into an account.

Cloud accounts: not connected in this preview

The account flow is disabled until a dedicated backend is configured and verified. In the planned free account, Supabase will process your email for sign-in and store the financial records you choose to enter (descriptions, dates, categories, amounts, bill reminders, savings and debts). No bank credentials or payment-card information is requested. The region, retention policy and final operator safeguards must be confirmed before accounts are launched.

When enabled, the dashboard uses email one-time codes and keeps the session in this tab’s memory, not browser local storage. It signs out on reload. Access policies restrict each account to its own records. You can correct or delete individual records. Linked entries must be removed first. Signing out clears records from the open dashboard, not from the account database. Supabase and the email-delivery provider may process technical logs needed to operate the service; do not enter bank passwords or unnecessary sensitive information in descriptions.

Hosting and service providers

The static website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical data such as IP addresses for security and operations. Read GitHub’s privacy statement. Before cloud accounts launch, the selected Supabase project and email provider must be reviewed and disclosed. See Supabase’s privacy policy.

Email updates and contact

The landing-page email field is not stored by this site. It opens a pre-written email; we receive it only if you send it. We use messages to reply and send updates you request. We do not sell financial records or use them for advertising. Do not email passwords, OTPs or financial records.

Your rights

Under the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173), you have rights to be informed, access, object, correct, request erasure, and complain to the National Privacy Commission. To request account deletion, data access or assistance, contact info@johnnybodegas.com. We must establish a verified identity and a retention/deletion schedule before account launch; no instant self-service account deletion is claimed here.

Links to other sites

Banks, e-wallets and other linked sites have their own privacy policies. Piso Wais does not connect to your bank account.
