Straight answers

FAQ

Is this financial advice?

No. Piso Wais shares education and tools. Your situation is yours; for decisions about big amounts, talk to a licensed professional.

Is the money in an e-wallet insured?

Not by itself. An e-wallet balance is not a bank deposit. Savings products like GSave (with CIMB Bank) or Maya Savings (with Maya Bank) are deposits in a bank. PDIC insures deposits up to PHP 1,000,000 per depositor, per bank. Check each product in the app.

Why do digital banks pay more than big banks?

They run without branches and compete for new depositors. Rates can change at any time, and some high numbers are promos with caps or spending missions. We show the base rate and the date we checked it.

Is the interest taxed?

Yes. Interest on peso deposits for individuals is subject to a 20% final withholding tax, deducted before it reaches you. Our calculator shows after-tax numbers.

Can a credit card really help my business?

Only if the purchase brings cash back before the due date and you pay in full. Then the card is a free 30 to 50 day float. If you carry a balance, interest of up to 3% a month (the BSP ceiling) can wipe out your margin. Use the Leverage Check before you swipe.

Do you get paid when I open an account?

Right now, no. Later, some buttons may use referral links. They will be labeled "Affiliate link". The rates and order on this site are never for sale.

Because the cheat sheet should help you either way. This site has no database. If you want updates, send us an email or follow @pisowaisph on Facebook.
