Credit, free lesson

3 ways to check your credit report in the Philippines, and which cost money

Bago mag-apply ng loan o card, silipin mo muna ang nakikita ng lender.

The Credit Information Corporation (CIC) keeps a central record of borrowers' credit data. You can request your own CIC credit report. Here are the routes CIC itself lists, what we could and could not confirm about cost, and what to do if something is wrong.

What is in a CIC credit report

According to CIC, your report contains basic information like your name, TIN, SSS or GSIS numbers, address, employer and business, plus your loan contracts, utility subscriptions and other obligations CIC is authorized to collect. Lenders can access it only with your written consent and for legitimate purposes.

2

Lesson 2 Free

Way 1: Through a CIC-accredited credit bureau app

CIC lists the Lista PH app (with CIBI Information) and the CreditMo app (with Island Credit Solutions) as ways to get your report. CIC's Lista PH page says it costs ₱199 per request (CIC page, as of Nov 4, 2026). We could not confirm CreditMo's price on a CIC page, so check inside the app.

3

Lesson 3 Free

Way 2: Email CRIF Philippines

CIC also lists CRIF Philippines, another accredited bureau, where you can email a request. Fees and requirements are set by CRIF, so ask in your email. Cost: Unverified.

4

Lesson 4 Free

Way 3: CIC Direct-to-Consumer program through authorized entities

Under CIC's Direct-to-Consumer (D2C) program, some banks, lenders and cooperatives can issue your CIC report, by email or walk-in. CIC says to prepare 1 to 2 valid government IDs and a "minimal service fee (depending on the AE)". Some of these entities only serve their existing borrowers, so check the CIC list first.

5

Lesson 5 Free

So, is there a free option?

Some legal commentaries say the law entitles borrowers to one free report a year. We could not confirm that on an official CIC page as of Nov 4, 2026, so treat it as Unverified. Ask CIC or the provider directly before you pay.

6

Lesson 6 Free

If something is wrong

CIC's D2C page says you can file a dispute online through CIC's dispute page using the transaction reference number on your report. Keep proof of payments, like receipts and certificates of full payment, to support your dispute.

Example Ramon, a jeepney operator in Iloilo, finished paying a motor loan two years ago, but his report still shows a balance. He keeps the lender's certificate of full payment and files a dispute with the reference number from his report.

Lesson 7 Free

How to read it

Check three things: your personal details are correct, every account listed is yours, and the balances and payment status match your own records. Keep your copy private. It contains sensitive data.

Your 7-day action

  1. Choose one of the three routes.
  2. Gather 1 to 2 valid IDs.
  3. Compare the report with your own records.

FAQ

Can I request my partner's report?

No. CIC says you cannot request another person's report unless you are an accredited entity with their written consent.

Can I opt out of CIC?

CIC says no. Credit data is submitted by lenders and service providers covered by RA 9510.

How often is it updated?

CIC says submitting entities update borrower data within 30 days of it being available to them.
