About Piso Wais

Simple money habits for Filipinos. Tips lang, hindi financial advice.

Piso Wais started as short videos about everyday money moves: paying yourself first, getting your idle cash to earn, using a credit card without letting it use you. The name says it: every piso, used wisely.

Who is behind it

Piso Wais is built by Johnny Bodegas, a Filipino entrepreneur based in Tagaytay who builds small businesses and online communities, including the Business Minded Filipinos community. Piso Wais is the money-habit side of that work: the practical lessons, written plainly, without hype.

What we believe

  • Habits beat hacks. Small, repeated moves change more than one lucky win.
  • Show the date. Rates change. Every number on this site says when we checked it and where it came from.
  • Say the risk out loud. Leverage multiplies losses too. If a tool says red, we mean it.
  • No fake proof. No invented testimonials or member counts. When we have real results, we will show them with permission.

What we are not

We are not a bank, lender, investment adviser or broker. We do not hold money or sell investments. Piso Wais is education. For big decisions, talk to a licensed professional.
