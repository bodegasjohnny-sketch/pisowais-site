## Compliance checklist
- [ ] Label every referral/affiliate link ("Affiliate link" tag already automatic) and keep the "How we are paid" note near platform cards.
- [ ] Use "#ad" / "Sponsored" / "Paid partnership" on sponsored social posts and in videos.
- [ ] "Educational only, not financial advice" on site, PDF, videos with money tips.
- [ ] Never promise returns or referral earnings; show rates with "as of" dates and "rates change, check the app".
- [ ] No insurance referrals without an Insurance Commission license.
- [ ] No investment solicitation; only SEC-registered products mentioned neutrally; check SEC advisories before mentioning any investment.
- [ ] Data Privacy Act (RA 10173): before collecting emails, publish a privacy notice describing purpose, storage, retention and rights; get consent; secure the data; register with NPC if thresholds apply.
- [ ] Respect each platform's referral T&C (some ban paid ads or brand-name bidding).
- [ ] Use logos only for identification; no implied endorsement.
- [ ] No fake testimonials or member counts.
